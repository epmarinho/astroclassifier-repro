# Dataset use and attribution

The AstroClassifier Dataset-v2 combines project-authored selection, labels,
splits, and derived representations with Digitized Sky Survey (DSS2) imagery.
The DSS imagery remains subject to the rights of its original institutions and
is not relicensed under the AstroClassifier source-code licence.

MAST states that scientists and educators may use its copyrighted DSS
collections for research, teaching, and other non-profit activities, with source
acknowledgement. Commercial or for-profit use requires written permission from
the relevant copyright holder. Copyright information for an individual product
is retained in its FITS header.

Users must consult:

- https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use
- https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/copyright.html
- https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/acknowledging.html

Publications using these data should acknowledge the Digitized Sky Surveys,
STScI, the Palomar and UK Schmidt source surveys as applicable, and CDS
HiPS2FITS. Suggested service credit: "This research made use of hips2fits, a
service provided by CDS."

Project-authored dataset compilation metadata may be reused for non-commercial
research with attribution to Eraldo Pereira Marinho and citation of the
associated AstroClassifier article. These terms do not override any FITS-header
notice or third-party right.
